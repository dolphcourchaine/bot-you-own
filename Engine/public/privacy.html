Who sees it

  • The owner of this site, in an admin area protected by a passphrase and, when switched on, an authenticator code.
  • Cloudflare, whose servers and database run the site, as a processor. The AI models that answer you also run there; your messages are sent to them to be answered and are not used to train anything.
  • The tools we use to email or text you, if you ticked those boxes: your email, name, number and what you agreed to are passed to them.
  • Nobody else. Nothing is sold. Nothing is shared with advertisers.

How long

Your sign-up, chats and tour progress: until you delete them. An attached file's text: 30 days. The owner's log of what was asked and answered: kept, without files. Server logs at Cloudflare: their standard retention.

Your choices

  • Delete my data, next to Sign out, erases everything about you on every bot here, at once. No form, no waiting.
  • The ✕ on a file chip removes that file from the conversation; the ✕ on a chat deletes that chat.
  • Unsubscribe from emails with the link in any email. Reply STOP to any text to stop texts.
  • This browser keeps a random key so the site recognises it. Clear site data, or use Sign out, and it forgets you.

The bots are AI

Answers are generated by a language model and can be wrong. Do not paste anything into a chat that you would not want kept for a while: card numbers, passwords, other people's private details. The site blocks what looks like a secret, but do not rely on that.

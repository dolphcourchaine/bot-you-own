# Fees

We publish two prices. Everything else is quoted after an exam, because it
depends on what we find.

- New patient exam, x-rays and clean: $189
- Emergency exam (existing patients): $95, which is credited against treatment

Crowns, fillings, whitening and orthodontic referrals are quoted after an exam.
We will always give you a written treatment plan with prices before any work
beyond the exam.

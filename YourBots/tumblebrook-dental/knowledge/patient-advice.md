## How often should I floss?
Once a day, at whatever time you'll actually do it. Most of our patients find
bedtime easiest. Brushing twice a day plus flossing once is the routine we
recommend for everyone; if your gums bleed a little when you start, keep going
gently for a week or two and it usually settles. If bleeding continues past two
weeks, book a check-up.

## I have a toothache. What do I do?
Call us on (303) 555-0142 during opening hours and say it's a toothache; we keep
same-day slots for pain. In the meantime: rinse with warm salt water, avoid very
hot, cold or sweet food on that side, and you can take an over-the-counter pain
reliever following the directions on the packet. Don't put aspirin directly on
the tooth or gum. If there is facial swelling, fever, or pain that keeps you from
sleeping, use the on-call line (303) 555-0199 outside hours.

## My tooth is sensitive to cold
Try a sensitivity toothpaste for two to three weeks. If it doesn't improve, or
the sensitivity is to heat as well, book a check-up; that can be a sign of
something we need to look at.

## I lost a filling or a crown came off
Keep the crown if you have it. Avoid chewing on that side and call us; we'll get
you in within a couple of days. A pharmacy temporary filling material is fine
to use in the meantime.

## My child knocked a baby tooth loose
Baby teeth are not re-implanted. Keep the area clean, give age-appropriate pain
relief, and call us the same day so we can check the adult tooth underneath.

## Do you do whitening?
Yes, take-home trays and in-chair whitening. Both are quoted after an exam
because whitening isn't suitable for every tooth.

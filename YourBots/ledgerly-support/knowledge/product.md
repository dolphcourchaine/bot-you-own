# Ledgerly — what it is

Ledgerly is bookkeeping software for solo consultants and freelancers. It
connects to your bank, categorizes transactions, sends invoices, and produces
the reports your accountant asks for at tax time.

## Bank connections
Ledgerly connects to over 11,000 banks in the US and Canada through Plaid.
Connections refresh automatically every night. If a connection breaks (usually
because your bank changed something), Ledgerly emails you and you reconnect in
two clicks under Settings → Banks.

## Invoicing
Create invoices, send them by email, and get paid by card or ACH. Card payments
cost 2.9% + 30¢; ACH costs 1% capped at $10. Recurring invoices are included on
every plan.

## Integrations
- Stripe (import payouts and fees)
- PayPal
- Gusto (payroll journal entries)
- Google Drive (receipt backup)
We do not currently integrate with QuickBooks or Xero. You can export everything
as CSV at any time.

# Plans and billing

## Plans
- **Solo** — $19/month. One business, unlimited invoices, bank connections,
  quarterly tax estimates.
- **Studio** — $39/month. Everything in Solo, plus up to 3 businesses, an
  accountant seat, and project profitability reports.

Both plans have a 14-day free trial. No card needed to start.

## Annual billing
Pay annually and get two months free: Solo $190/year, Studio $390/year.

## Refunds
We refund the current month in full if you cancel within 30 days of your first
payment, no questions asked. After that, cancelling stops the next charge but we
don't refund the current period. Annual plans can be refunded pro-rata within the
first 30 days only.

## Discounts
We don't offer coupons, promo codes or negotiated discounts. The annual plan is
the only discount, and everyone gets it.

## Cancelling
Settings → Billing → Cancel. Takes effect at the end of the current period. Your
data stays exportable for 90 days after that.

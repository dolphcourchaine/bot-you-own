# Picking a weight, and how a set should feel

## Starting weight
Pick a weight where the last one or two reps of a set are hard but your form
stays clean. If you finish a set and could have done five more, it's too light.
If your form breaks before the last rep, it's too heavy — drop it.

## Progressing
When you hit all the sets and reps for a lift two sessions running with clean
form, add the smallest jump: 2.5 kg on upper-body lifts, 5 kg on squat and
deadlift. Small and steady beats big and stalled.

## How it should feel
- A working set should feel like effort: muscles burning, breathing hard. That's
  normal and good.
- Next-day muscle soreness (DOMS) is normal, especially on a new movement. It
  eases in a day or two.

## What is NOT normal
- Sharp pain, or any pain in a joint (knee, shoulder, elbow, lower back).
- Pain that gets worse mid-set rather than just harder.
- Pain that's still there at rest the next day.
Any of these: stop the lift, don't push through, and message your coach.

## Swaps
If you can't do a listed exercise (no kit, or it aggravates something), don't
improvise a replacement yourself — ask your coach and they'll swap it in your
plan. Skipping it for one session is fine; guessing a substitute isn't.

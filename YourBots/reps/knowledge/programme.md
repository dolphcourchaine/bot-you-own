# The Vity Strength programme (3-day)

Everyone on the standard plan runs three sessions a week: Push, Pull, Legs, in
any order with a rest day between where you can.

## Push day
- Barbell bench press — 4 sets of 6
- Overhead press — 3 sets of 8
- Incline dumbbell press — 3 sets of 10
- Triceps rope pushdown — 3 sets of 12
- Rest 90 seconds between sets on the first two, 60 seconds on the rest.

## Pull day
- Deadlift — 4 sets of 5
- Barbell row — 4 sets of 8
- Lat pulldown — 3 sets of 10
- Face pull — 3 sets of 15
- Biceps curl — 3 sets of 12
- Rest 2 minutes after deadlift sets, 60–90 seconds on the rest.

## Legs day
- Back squat — 4 sets of 6
- Romanian deadlift — 3 sets of 8
- Leg press — 3 sets of 12
- Walking lunge — 3 sets of 10 each leg
- Calf raise — 3 sets of 15
- Rest 2 minutes after squats, 90 seconds on the rest.

## Warm-up (every session)
5 minutes easy cardio, then two light warm-up sets of the first lift before your
working weight.

# Northwind Joinery — the basics

Northwind Joinery makes fitted furniture — wardrobes, alcove units, window seats and
bookcases — for homes within 40 miles of Boulder, Colorado. Established 2011. Eleven
people: six in the workshop, three fitters, two in the office.

## Opening hours
The workshop and office are open Monday to Friday, 8:00am to 4:00pm Mountain time.
Closed weekends and public holidays. Survey visits can be booked for a Saturday
morning by arrangement, but nobody answers the phone that day.

## Where we work
Anywhere within 40 miles of Boulder at no extra charge. Between 40 and 70 miles we add
a travel charge of $85 per fitting day. Beyond 70 miles we do not take work on.

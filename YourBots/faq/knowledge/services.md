# What we make, what it costs, how long it takes

## Lead times
From the day the deposit clears:
- Alcove units and bookcases: 4 to 5 weeks
- Fitted wardrobes: 6 to 8 weeks
- Whole-room fit-outs: 10 to 12 weeks

Lead times run about two weeks longer between September and November, which is our
busiest stretch.

## Prices
A survey visit is free within 40 miles. Typical finished prices, fitted:
- A pair of alcove units: $2,400 to $3,800
- A three-door fitted wardrobe: $4,900 to $7,200
- A window seat with drawers: $1,950 to $2,600

Every quote is fixed once the survey is done. We ask for a 40% deposit to book a slot,
with the balance due on the day the fitting is finished.

## Delivery and fitting
Delivery and fitting are included in every quoted price within 40 miles. We do not sell
flat-pack or supply-only — everything we make, we fit.

## Guarantee
Ten years on the carcass and the joinery, two years on moving parts (hinges, runners,
soft-close mechanisms). The guarantee covers workmanship, not damage or misuse, and it
transfers to a new owner if the house is sold.

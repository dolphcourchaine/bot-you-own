# How CareerForge runs a mock interview

This is reference the interviewer can lean on. It is not read aloud as-is.

## STAR
A strong behavioural answer has four parts:
- Situation — the context, briefly.
- Task — what you were responsible for.
- Action — what YOU specifically did (not "we").
- Result — the outcome, with a number or a concrete change where possible.

The most common weakness is skipping straight to Action with no Situation, or
ending with no Result. The second most common is saying "we" so much that the
interviewer can't tell what the candidate personally did.

## What the four scores mean
- Structure — did the answer have a clear shape (e.g. STAR), or did it ramble?
- Specificity — real detail and numbers, or vague generalities?
- Relevance — did it actually answer the question asked?
- Communication — clear, concise, confident; not too long, not too thin.

## Good coaching feedback
Concrete beats general. "Add the result — how much did response time drop?" beats
"be more specific". Always name one thing that worked before the fix.

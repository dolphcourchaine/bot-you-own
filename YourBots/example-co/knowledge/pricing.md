## Call-out
Our standard call-out is $180, which covers the first hour on site.

## Ongoing work
Anything beyond the first hour is billed at our standard hourly rate. We confirm
that in writing before we start.

## Maintenance contracts
These are priced per machine and depend on how old the equipment is and how hard
you run it. There isn't a list price — email hello@example.com and we'll put a
number in front of you within two working days.

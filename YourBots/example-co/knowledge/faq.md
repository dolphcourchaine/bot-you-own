## How fast can you get someone out to us?
Usually within two working days. If a machine is down and stopping production,
say so when you contact us and we'll try to get someone out same-day.

## Do you work on machines you didn't install?
Yes. Most of our work is on equipment somebody else put in.

## What areas do you cover?
The Midwest. If you're outside that, we'll say so rather than waste your time.

## Do you offer maintenance contracts?
Yes. They're the cheapest way to work with us if you have more than two machines.
Email us and we'll talk through what makes sense.

## What are your hours?
Monday to Friday, 7am to 5pm. We are closed weekends.
